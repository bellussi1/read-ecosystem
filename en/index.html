Built in the open.

Individual Kindle transfers have been tested. The full KOReader–Hardcover flow, batch transfers and sidecar recovery still need real-device validation. The Windows installer is unsigned.

Good to know

Do I need a Hardcover account?

No account is needed to browse and organize a Calibre library. Hardcover features require an account.

Does it sync with Goodreads or StoryGraph?

Not directly. Reviewable CSV import is available for both services.

Where are my books stored?

Your books remain in your Calibre library on your computer.

Is it free?

Yes. KOReader Ecosystem is open source under the MIT license.
