04 / GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a Hardcover account?

Not to browse and organize your Calibre library. An account is needed only for features that use Hardcover.

Does the app sync automatically with Goodreads or StoryGraph?

No. Reviewable CSV import is available; direct synchronization with those services is not currently supported.

Where are my books stored?

Your files remain in your Calibre library. The app accesses your local library and can send an ebook to a Kindle connected over USB.

Is the project free?

Yes. KOReader Ecosystem is open source under the MIT license.
